This notebook describes our participation in the PAN 2026 Voight-Kampff Generative AI Detection task.
We propose RADAR (Robust Adversarial-Resistant Detection with Adaptive Reasoning), a hybrid architecture
that combines a deep contextual encoder with a handcrafted stylometric feature module, bridged by a
cross-attention fusion layer.

\textbf{System:} RADAR is submitted as a Docker image via the Tira platform.
\textbf{Code:} \url{https://github.com/Mahmood-Anaam/radar}
\textbf{Models:} \url{https://huggingface.co/Mahmood-Anaam/RADAR}
